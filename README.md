One image summing up the world's news of Monday, 23 November 2026:
**45 dated stories, 7 regions, every one verified, real news photographs
only.** Newspaper front page — masthead, numbers strip, region bar, UTC
timeline, 3 photo leads, 2 more photo stories, 10 medium cards, a world
map with 13 stories pinned, 30 more stories, and a date-check box listing
the wrong-day stories deliberately left out.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123)
news-image-20261123 bounty. `main.ts` copies `entry.png` (1024×1024,
deterministic) to the output directory; `draw_image.py` (Pillow) drew the
front page from the 5 real news photographs in `photos/`.
